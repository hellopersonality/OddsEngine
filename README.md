# Odds Engine

A juicy, neo-brutalist chance machine for the browser. One play sets the whole machine in motion: dice tumble and land, spill piles of coins, and the coins flip one by one into your score.

## How it plays
- **Level 1** starts you with a d6 in the first lineup slot and free plays (no limit). Fill the bar to level up. The first level-up offers no legendaries yet, so the first pick stays easy to read.
- **Level ups** offer three upgrade cards (Common / Rare / Legendary), survivor-style. Rerolls are earned every 4 levels.
- **Randomizers** (d6 to d20 dice) take up limited slots (5 to start). When full, a new die is swapped in for an old one.
- **Paylines:** any straight run of 3+ coins in the layout counts: across, down or along either diagonal. All heads pays a multiplier. When a line is one coin away, the machine pauses for a tease.
- **Pouch:** plain coins are unlimited; the pouch holds your special coins (golden, lead, wild, ember, magnet, crown), won from level-up cards. Every special you own joins exactly once per play at a random spot, and plain coins fill the rest. Specials that react to their neighbours resolve in an effects step after the flips.
- **Mirror coin:** instead of flipping, it copies the coin its arrow points at (heads or tails, value and all) in the effects step.
- **Effect dice:** special dice from level-up cards that take a slot like any die. Their marked faces (shown in the die's accent colour, with a spinning ring when they land) trigger once every coin has settled: Re-flip d6 (evens: every tails flips again), Spinner d8 (7–8: the board spins like slot reels), Wild d4 (3–4: a tails turns wild), Midas d10 (9–10: a tails turns golden), Gamble d12 (12 doubles the press, 1 halves it).
- **No hidden effects:** every machine-wide upgrade (Hot streak, Second chance, Lucky coins, Paylines, Perfectionist) shows as a square token in the row under the XP bar, with live values during a play (current streak bonus, re-flips left). Parts sit as hex nuts on the right; die upgrades are badges under each die. Tap a token or part for the Machine card, or a die for its card.
- **Main menu and install:** the game opens on a main menu (Continue / New run / Install app), also reachable from Options. Odds Engine is an installable web app (manifest, icons, offline service worker in sw.js). Install app shows steps for the detected device and browser (iPhone/iPad Safari incl. iOS 26's ··· → Share menu, Chrome/Firefox on iOS, Android Chrome/Samsung/Firefox, desktop Chrome/Edge/Safari), or a one-tap Install button where the browser supports it. On phones it's offered once, right after the first level-up pick.
- **Play button (coin-op box):** PLAY is a coin-op box in the middle of the tray, with your plays as a stack of tokens in a tray at its foot (refunded plays are blue tokens with a ring, the last one is red, level 1 is a bottomless ∞ stack). Swipe the top token up into the slot (it follows your finger; flick it or drag it most of the way and it shoots in, a harder flick hits harder; let go early and it drops back), or tap the box (or Space / Enter) and the top token flies in by itself, before the machine runs. On touch screens a SWIPE UP callout shows on the first plays until you've swiped 3 times (or tapped 5). The box steps aside during a play (the coins and dice take the stage, on level 1 too) and comes back alone once the board clears. A token counter under the XP bar always shows plays left; tokens refunded mid-play (Rebate) fly into it. A new level drops fresh tokens onto the stack one by one.
- **Play cap:** you can hold at most 5 plays. Free plays from Rebate past that are ignored (a PLAYS FULL pop-up shows it).
- **Die upgrades are per die:** Exploding dice, Rebate, Loaded dice and Max roll bonus upgrade every die you own at the moment you pick them. Dice added later start plain; picking the upgrade again catches them up (and levels up the others). Swapping a die in clears that slot's upgrades; Polyhedral keeps them.
- **Exploding dice:** an upgrade where a max roll bursts and rolls again, adding to the coin count (up to 3 times in a row at max level).
- **Machine parts:** rule-changers from level-up cards, 3 slots, swapped out when full. Cascade (paid lines drop away and refill), Slot reels (when two dice match, the board spins column by column after the flips and any new lines pay), Lucky sevens (dice totals divisible by 7 pay ×2), Overclock (double coins, one fewer play), Echo chamber (first line pays double), Safety net (bottom-row tails flip again). They sit as hex nuts beside the play counters and punch when they fire; the pouch lists them.
- **Charms:** keyring tags from level-up cards that share the lineup slots with your dice. Once a play has settled they fire left to right on the play's total (a running total shows in the tray), so order matters: + charms before × charms. Some read the nearest die to their left (skipping other charms, so one die can set off a chain) and light their badge when it rolls right (High Roller ≥5 ×2, Underdog 1–2 ×3, Even Keel, Thermometer heats up +0.2× for good on 5+); others read the board or the lineup (Penny Jar, Tip Jar, Lucky Pair (any two dice match ×2), Four-Leaf Clover, Horseshoe, Anchor ×3 if last, Bell fires its left neighbour again, Copycat copies its right neighbour). Drag any die or charm to rearrange the lineup between plays; tap one for its card.
- **Upgrade cards:** each type has its own silhouette and coloured pill: dice have chamfered corners, charms are keyring tags, coins have a struck arch (a round end on phones), machine parts are riveted plates, and plain upgrades are slabs. A gem marks rarity (blue rare, gold legendary with a rainbow edge), and the footer says where the card goes. Tap the type pill for an explainer of that type.
- **Play budget:** from level 2, every level gets 5 plays. Run out short of the goal and the run ends.

## Controls
- Swipe a token up into the coin-op slot, tap the box, or press Space / Enter
- Level-up cards: tap, or press 1 / 2 / 3; R to reroll
- Options: juice on/off (for comparing feel), slow-mo, sound, light/dark mode, reset

## Running it
It's a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser, or serve it with GitHub Pages.

## Updates
`version.json` holds the current build id, and the same id is baked into `index.html`. An open game checks `version.json` every couple of minutes (and when the tab comes back to the foreground). When the ids differ, it offers a one-tap **Update**. Run `./bump.sh` before each commit that changes the game, so both get a fresh id.
